Sylvie the Dove
A dove animatronic I created for the UNCSA spring opera La Colombe (The Dove), as part of the show: Of Sorcery and Songbirds. I puppeteered the dove during the show using a control box with an ESP-32 that could connect via ESP-now. For this project, I sculpted and fabricated a macquette to be used as color and scale reference. I then designed, fabricated, and assembled the internal mechanics with plastic shells. Fur and feathers were placed on top of the shells to create the appearance of a real dove.

Skills: Sculpting, Mechanical Design, Electronics, Puppetery.
